# Tennis match dates, courts and weather alignment

Research audit and hosted-result update: 2026-09-25. This document contains source contracts, aggregate results and proposed diagnostics, not match or weather records. Research used primary publisher pages, repository code and published aggregate reports/manifests. No datasets were downloaded locally. No new Open-Meteo or Overpass requests were made.

The bounded Wimbledon pilot ran twice and currently has **zero accepted match joins and zero weather-context rows**. It validates source access and a current facility/coordinate mapping, but does not establish match-level weather coverage. A reusable historical feed of actual match start/end times, session assignments and match-specific roof operation was **not verified**. Any future accepted output must remain calendar-day context, with explicit missing timing, rather than playing-hour weather exposure.

## Bounded entity request and strict mapping

Use `https://www.wikidata.org/w/api.php` with `action=wbgetentities`, `ids=Q128304298|Q121076423|Q2603183|Q41520|Q815369`, `props=info|labels|aliases|claims`, **`languages=en|mul`**, `format=json` and `maxlag=5`. A second bounded request may resolve the participants and tournament-edition IDs actually returned. The [publisher's default-term documentation](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Help:Default_values_for_labels_and_aliases) assigns default labels and aliases to `mul`; the [Action API language filter](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Presenting_Wikidata_knowledge) can otherwise omit those terms. The corrected collector preserves both requested languages and selected-language provenance, without hardcoded participant names. Never expand recursively or switch to a broad SPARQL crawl. Stop on access denial or budget exhaustion and publish an aggregate reason.

Candidate prerequisites are validated immediately after the entity requests. Each rejected candidate retains its reason; if none pass, publish CC0/audit output without fetching either archive. A rejected 2023 candidate cannot block an otherwise valid 2024 candidate.

Read `entities[Q].claims` for `P585` (match date), `P710` (participants), `P276` (location), `P361` (part-of edition/facility), `P31` (event type) and `P625` (Earth coordinate). Preserve claim IDs, ranks, qualifiers, reference counts, entity `lastrevid`, modification time, retrieval time and response SHA-256. Ignore deprecated claims; reject unknown values, conflicting dates, ambiguous locations and unsupported calendar models. Do not derive dates from the tournament's start-of-week field. Entity modification after a match is not proof the information existed before that match.

[Wikidata's date model](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Help:Dates) defines precision 11 as a calendar day. A serialized midnight with a `Z` suffix is not an observed UTC match start. Preserve the source precision and date, and keep actual start/end and session fields null. Duration cannot recover either endpoint. Require exactly one supported day, two distinct resolved participants and an unambiguous court for an accepted match candidate.

Join to MCP only when men's-singles competition, tournament edition/year, final round, calendar day and the unordered full participant-name pair all agree uniquely. Normalize Unicode, whitespace and case conservatively; any accent/punctuation aliases need explicit auditable resolution. Do not match on initials, surnames alone, winner, score or point outcome. Missing or ambiguous identities stay excluded with a reason. The 2023 entity can produce zero accepted rows without making the run a failure.

For weather, use only the already identified Wimbledon coordinate key. Verify the fetched court's facility and the weather entity's facility are the same Q815369 identity. Confirm the archive's requested coordinate agrees with the fetched Wimbledon coordinate claim; allow only a documented rounding tolerance. Separately inspect court-coordinate distance as a sanity check, with a predeclared small facility-scale bound such as 500 metres. Distance is a rejection check, never a mechanism to pick another weather location. Conflicting coordinates or missing shared-facility evidence must produce no weather join. Preserve the different court, tournament-coordinate and ERA5 grid identities; the result is a same-facility weather proxy, not a court sensor. Current coordinate snapshots retain unknown historical validity.

## Calendar-day interpretation and leakage limits

Use `Europe/London` only as an explicitly declared venue-timezone mapping, and separately record the assumption that the corroborated match date is the local playing date. Convert the bounds of that **calendar day**, not an invented match time, to find overlapping existing UTC daily buckets. For summer dates the local day overlaps two UTC buckets. Keep their individual values and overlap durations separate. A weighted average of daily means does not recover the local day's weather because within-day variation is unknown; daily maxima, rainfall and wind summaries also cannot become match-hour observations.

Name the result `calendar_day_overlap_context`; record `actual_match_interval_known=false`, `roof_operation_known=false`, `pregame_available=false`, `automatic_training_join_allowed=false` and the distinct timezone/date assumptions. The calendar boundary timestamps must never populate match-start/end columns. Suspended or multi-day matches require separate actual-play-date evidence and should be excluded from this one-day pilot. No intrapoint time, weather causality, execution advantage or profitability can be inferred.

Publish CC0 entity facts separately from the MCP-derived noncommercial join. Preserve the [MCP source's CC BY-NC-SA restrictions](https://github.com/JeffSackmann/tennis_MatchChartingProject), weather attribution and the existing free-API noncommercial terms. The assembled research cohort does not become commercially reusable because one input is CC0. Keep the existing pre-2024 fit / 2024 calibration / 2025-plus holdout labels if later analysis is authorized; this task does not train or tune a model.

## Remaining verified gaps and unsuitable shortcuts

The official [dated Wimbledon order of play PDF](https://www.wimbledon.com/en_GB/scores/2024/schedule/pdf/schedulePDF20.pdf) distinguishes an intended schedule from actual play. Its print/update timestamp is not a match end time, and a first-court scheduled start is not the start of every following match. A historical open redistribution license was not verified. The unversioned current schedule URL must not be treated as a 2024 archive.

The tennis-data.co.uk data/notes pages returned 403 during this audit; no bypass was attempted and neither historical schema nor redistribution rights were established. A newer aggregate repository's [data-license notice](https://github.com/ryantjx/tennis-match-data/blob/main/DATA_LICENSE.md) explicitly separates software licensing from upstream data permissions; it is not an open-license shortcut for TennisTV/WTA material.

No generally reusable pre-2025 match-court attendance series or actual roof-operation feed was verified. Official tournament day/session totals count a different population from spectators at one match, and current court capacity does not establish historical occupancy. The proposed pilot should report accepted match identities, court/facility checks, weather-bucket coverage and exclusion counts only; it should make no attendance or roof inference.

## Verified frozen-input diagnosis and remaining gaps

Read-only code review identified a specific possible coverage limitation: `resolved_aliases()` selects the first nonempty English alias list and then ignores the `mul` list for matching, although both are fetched and exported. A fabricated in-memory example confirmed that a full-name default alias can be omitted when another English alias exists. Wikidata's default-term documentation says aliases need not be repeated in every language. The hosted diagnostic found **zero omitted eligible aliases for these frozen candidates**; this behavior does not explain their failed match. No real participant rows were inspected locally, and accepted matching remains unchanged.

The [verified diagnostic release](TENNIS_ALIGNMENT_DIAGNOSTIC_RELEASE.md) compares 7,564 normalized and 7,566 source-export men's metadata rows. All 492 supported Wimbledon rows have parseable calendar dates, but there are zero rows on the prerequisite-valid candidate's recorded exact day in either representation. Seven exact participant-pair rows elsewhere cannot substitute for that date. Both alias policies remain at zero matches. All 7,564 shared IDs retain the same literal identity fields and canonical dates. A separate conflicting source ID accounts for two excluded rows and one globally invalid source date; it does not contain a raw exact-day candidate removed by normalization.

The completed diagnostic publishes independent and cumulative gate counts, first-failure counts, ambiguity checks, alias-policy comparisons and raw-versus-normalized integrity counts in CSV and aggregate JSON. Thirty synthetic checks and independent identity/transport reviews passed. The hosted run verified both frozen archives and transferred 45,966,624 bytes in eight requests with no errors or live source refresh. Accepted match/weather joins remain zero; no outcomes or prices entered identity resolution.

This is an exact-day coverage gap in the pinned snapshot, not proof of global source absence or the correctness of an external date claim. Before another alignment collection, independently audit actual date semantics and source coverage. Do not replay unchanged archives, relax dates, or use scores/winners to obtain a match. Keep actual data processing on the runner; local inspection remains limited to aggregate reports. Any change to accepted matching needs source-contract justification and review. The valid zero-join weather audit remains the result until evidence resolves the gap.
