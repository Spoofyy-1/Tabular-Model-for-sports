# Weather, attendance and player-performance joins

Audit date: 2026-09-25. This review used repository code, primary documentation and small published aggregate reports/manifests only. No game rows, weather rows or source archives were downloaded locally. No collector or workflow was changed.

NBA reported attendance is the most immediately joinable new context. NFL already has game-level weather and roof fields. Tennis weather is collected but does not yet have defensible match/venue/time alignment. None of these facts establishes a causal player response or betting profitability.

## NBA: a schedule-only route under 120 MB

`context/nba_context.py` normalizes ESPN game IDs, scheduled start/date precision, source-local date, opponent IDs, venue ID/name, completion status, `attendance_actual` and `venue_capacity_source`. The [primary schedule release](https://github.com/sportsdataverse/sportsdataverse-data/releases/tag/espn_nba_schedules) supplies season-ending-year Parquet assets. The upstream [loader code](https://github.com/sportsdataverse/hoopR/blob/main/R/load_nba.R) and existing normalization establish a direct ESPN-ID route to the base NBA player-game rows. A future hosted join still must validate uniqueness, date, opponents and completion rather than trusting namespace compatibility alone.

The previously published [NBA context aggregate report](https://github.com/kennynakao/Tabular-Model-for-sports/releases/download/context-36094215424-1/nba_context_summary.json) contains every original schedule URL, SHA-256 and byte count in `source_assets`, filtered by `dataset=schedules`. The report itself hashes to `83bc304e188265c7d74517b73f9cee2706260020c578e141715ac10f8eab0f14`. Its 25 schedule assets, seasons 2002–2026, total **2,979,582 bytes**. The 2002–2025 subset totals **2,453,780 bytes**. Include the 2025 season-ending file to capture autumn 2024 games; filter actual UTC dates rather than assuming season year equals game year.

For concrete source pins, [nba_schedule_2024.parquet](https://github.com/sportsdataverse/sportsdataverse-data/releases/download/espn_nba_schedules/nba_schedule_2024.parquet) is 143,706 bytes, SHA-256 `2069783562b531b91ee9ad6a85358761ad6a611ce3291a60ed124bb978969b63`. [nba_schedule_2025.parquet](https://github.com/sportsdataverse/sportsdataverse-data/releases/download/espn_nba_schedules/nba_schedule_2025.parquet) is 151,821 bytes, SHA-256 `239829fdcf99629ee682bca4d6d0a6709b3c095be39c39f425664754ae1a979e`. These are recorded historical pins, not a promise mutable upstream assets still match. A hash mismatch must stop that input and be reported.

The [base NBA manifest](https://github.com/kennynakao/Tabular-Model-for-sports/releases/download/csv-36170200182-1/base-nba_csv_asset_manifest.json) pins `csv-base-nba.tar.gz` to 103,971,052 bytes and SHA-256 `5642335016aca6e0d39d7cb1c7c9f74b989de74dd2fcb2217fed4046e1bef3b7`. Its [archive URL](https://github.com/kennynakao/Tabular-Model-for-sports/releases/download/csv-36170200182-1/csv-base-nba.tar.gz), plus the 2002–2025 schedules, totals **106,424,832 compressed bytes** before small metadata. That fits a later 120 MB hosted source budget. Do not redownload the 483,596,480-byte combined NBA context CSV archive merely to obtain schedules. A new schedule-only command is needed because the current context CLI also downloads play-by-play.

The aggregate report records 32,290 unique scheduled games, with 9,262 missing capacity values. Attendance has zero null entries in that normalized snapshot; this does **not** establish valid attendance for every game. Audit zero/sentinel values, unplayed games, unusual neutral venues and exceptional attendance regimes. Preserve raw reported attendance separately from any quality-qualified measure. Source rights remain attributed hoopR CC BY 4.0 with the distribution repository's MIT notice; do not discard the original notices.

Capacity is a retrospective provider value with no effective-from/effective-to record here. Renovations, basketball seating layouts, temporary venues, restrictions and standing-room policies can change the denominator. A positive attendance/capacity division is only a **reported-capacity ratio candidate** until denominator validity is established. Ratios above one should be flagged, not clipped; zero must not automatically mean a confirmed empty stadium. Neither attendance nor capacity measures crowd noise, density near the court, allegiance or fan enthusiasm.

## NFL: weather is available; crowd counts remain a gap

The existing NFL base schedule preserves source fields, including `temp`, `wind`, `roof` and `stadium`. The producer's [dataset documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nflverse/nfldata/master/DATASETS.md) defines Eastern-time kickoff, neutral-site distinctions, roof categories including open/closed retractable roofs, and outdoor/open-roof temperature and wind. Wind is documented in miles per hour. Confirm each field's units and coverage from the actual pinned schema before cross-sport conversion. A missing indoor temperature or wind is not zero weather exposure.

This documented schedule contract does not establish game attendance or historical capacity. Official NFL gamebooks are a possible exact-game source, but an [official club-hosted gamebook](https://static.clubs.nfl.com/image/upload/chargers/apac14ebrrha9ui8dbny) explicitly limits non-media reuse without written NFL permission. Do not build an automated gamebook ingestion pipeline by assuming a public PDF grants reuse rights. Paid attendance also differs from measured people in seats. No suitable openly licensed complete NFL attendance/capacity history was verified in this bounded audit.

## Tennis: do not join date strings directly

The existing [tennis aggregate report](https://github.com/kennynakao/Tabular-Model-for-sports/releases/download/extras-36173901119-1/tennis_context_summary.json) reports **29,953 daily weather rows for seven coordinate pairs**, covering 2015-01-01 through 2026-09-18. The source place catalog contains 50 distinct places. These are collection counts, not match coverage. Its current Wikidata capacity values lack historical validity qualifiers in our extraction and cannot supply a verified match-level occupancy denominator.

Existing weather requests explicitly use ERA5 and GMT. [Open-Meteo documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api) defines daily variables as 24-hour aggregates and ERA5 at roughly 25 km resolution. The [MCP dictionary](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JeffSackmann/tennis_MatchChartingProject/master/data_dictionary.txt) identifies point sequence and score, but supplies no point wall-clock series. A match calendar date cannot be treated as a UTC timestamp or used to select its playing-hour weather. A full UTC day's maximum temperature or rainfall may include conditions after play ended.

A defensible later join needs a verified tournament-year facility, an actual match date with timezone semantics, court assignment, and match start/end or session timing. Suspended matches can span days. A verified indoor match should not receive an outdoor-exposure interpretation. Retractable-roof capability, weather notes or historical OSM infrastructure do not establish the roof's operation during that match. Preserve unresolved joins and the reason; never substitute noon or a tournament-week start.

Official tournament attendance can have the wrong unit: the [USTA's dated opening-day report](https://www.usopen.org/amp/en_US/news/articles/2024-08-26/opening_day_of_the_2024_us_open_sets_singleday_attendance_record.html) reports day/night-session crowds. Such grounds/session totals are not attendance at each simultaneously played match. Do not divide them by one court's capacity. A general historical match-court crowd-count source remains unverified.

For descriptive player-performance comparisons, contemporaneous attendance and realized weather can be clearly labeled retrospective context. Pregame features require only previously available information: observed target-game attendance and reanalysis are not historical forecasts. Keep pre-2024 fit, 2024 calibration and 2025-plus holdout boundaries, and preserve the existing CC BY-NC-SA MCP and weather API noncommercial restrictions. No training is part of this audit.
