# Coach appointment announcement CSVs

The hosted release [coach-events-36182978719-1](https://github.com/kennynakao/Tabular-Model-for-sports/releases/tag/coach-events-36182978719-1) contains five NFL announcement annotations: three appointments and two agreements to terms. Three announcement dates are resolved; two remain unknown because the announcement weekday and source publication calendar date do not agree. A timezone difference may explain such a mismatch, but the collector does not invent a timezone or effective employment date to resolve it.

Eight primary team pages were requested, transferring 2,221,245 source bytes on the hosted runner. All five NFL pages supplied qualifying evidence. The three NBA pages returned HTTP 403 and were not retried or bypassed. These are access gaps, not evidence that the appointments did not happen. The source catalog and research distinctions appear in [the coaching source audit](COACH_TENURE_SOURCE_AUDIT.md).

The compressed CSV `data/coach_events/appointments.csv.gz` is packaged in `csv-coach-appointments.tar.gz`. Rows are sorted by announcement date, with unknown dates last, then sport, team and source ID. Identifiers remain strings and nulls use `\N`. Schema, source audit, SHA-256 manifest, aggregate summary and rights notes accompany the table. Source hashes explicitly describe decoded UTF-8 text; evidence hashes describe normalized article blocks. No article bodies, quotes, images or headlines are redistributed.

Canonical coach/team IDs, first game coached and effective employment start/end dates remain null. Historical publication availability is not independently verified, and no annotation is automatically joined to a training game. Appointment and agreement records are separate event types. A current source label is not a verified complete tenure or an explanation of coaching style. This small selected catalog is not complete league coverage.

Twenty-four synthetic checks passed before collection and in hosted CI. They cover primary-article scoping, nested recommendation and article exclusion, modification/publication distinctions, conflicting dates, redirected story identity, same-origin trailing-slash handling, bounded transfers and cloud guards. The eight-source collector is capped at 20 requests and 20 MB. All actual ingestion and CSV creation occurred on GitHub-hosted Actions; GPU training remains paused.
