# NFL weather and player-performance CSVs

The successful [weather-performance-36184196625-1 release](https://github.com/kennynakao/Tabular-Model-for-sports/releases/tag/weather-performance-36184196625-1) joins 475,586 source player/game rows to 7,273 games, with no rejected game/team/opponent/date/season joins. It reuses the pinned NFL base CSV archive, transferring 88,715,353 bytes only on GitHub-hosted Actions.

Development through calendar 2024 contains 454,283 rows; 2025 onward contains 21,303. This descriptive export separates development and holdout; it does not fit parameters or calibrate a model. The larger counts than the original model-ready table reflect the retained source player cohort, including defensive/special-teams roles and rows that the baseline model's eligibility screen excludes. Do not treat every source player row as an offered prop or independent weather experiment.

Open-air player rows with reported wind and temperature total 340,486. Roof groups contain 348,831 outdoor rows, 77,512 dome rows, 40,835 closed-retractable rows and 8,408 open-retractable rows. Indoor/closed/unknown roof states are kept separate from open-air weather buckets; missing weather is not converted to calm wind or zero temperature. Current-game weather and player outcomes are retrospective observations, not verified historical forecasts or pregame inputs.

The release contains 8,280 descriptive comparison rows grouped by development/holdout, source position, roof category, temperature-assumption bucket, wind bucket and metric. Passing/rushing/receiving outcomes retain raw player-game values and are summarized with means, medians, sums, distinct games/players, nonmissing counts, missing counts and paired positive-opportunity denominators. These unadjusted summaries do not control for roster, opponent, venue, usage, season or player selection and do not estimate a causal weather effect or betting edge.

The source dictionary explicitly documents wind in mph, converted to km/h by multiplying by 1.609344. Its temperature description does not independently establish Fahrenheit. Accordingly, every temperature conversion and temperature bucket is explicitly labelled `assuming_fahrenheit`, with `temperature_unit_independently_verified=false`; raw values remain available. A Fahrenheit-to-Celsius calculation is an assumption, not a verified source unit. The [primary schedule dictionary](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nflverse/nflreadr/main/data-raw/dictionary_schedules.csv) and source attribution are retained in the release notes/schema.

`csv-nfl-weather-performance.tar.gz` contains separate compressed CSVs for development and holdout, descriptive associations, an empty exclusion audit, schema, provenance and source-rights documents. IDs remain strings, timestamps remain UTC, and nulls use `\N`. Archive and member hashes and original CSV schemas were checked on the runner. Eleven synthetic tests and hosted coverage gates passed. No model training occurred, and no attendance or crowding was inferred.
