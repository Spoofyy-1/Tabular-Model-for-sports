# Remaining data gaps

The context expansion collects additional observed sports records. It does not turn every column into an eligible pregame predictor. Prioritize data that describes expected participation, opportunity and matchup strength over arbitrary correlations.

1. **NBA injury announcements with report times.** The [official NBA injury report](https://official.nba.com/nba-injury-report-2025-26-season/) and the [nbainjuries parser](https://github.com/mxufc29/nbainjuries) are candidate sources. The parser's software license does not independently establish the completeness or redistribution conditions of every historical report. A future collector must preserve report publication time, target game, player identity, revisions and source URL; exclude reports published after the decision time. No NBA injury archive is claimed in this release.
2. **Player ID crosswalk and expected NBA defender.** The added matchup tables identify actual defenders using official NBA IDs. The boxscore model uses ESPN IDs. Resolve player identities with source-backed IDs, then derive earlier-game defender tendencies. A target game's realized defender and defensive possessions are outcomes, not pregame inputs.
3. **Historical executable prop quotes.** Existing quote samples do not provide a complete tradable panel. The project still needs line, side, offered price or lineup payout, product, observation time, event start, eligibility/void rules, fees and realistic fills. See [historical odds](HISTORICAL_ODDS.md) and [expansion audit](ODDS_EXPANSION.md). No paid subscription was purchased.
4. **Expected minutes, snaps and injury returns.** An absent boxscore is not automatically zero. Participation probability and workload need their own labels and pregame evidence. The new tracking and depth-chart data can help once their availability and revision history are audited.
5. **Travel, weather and other context.** Schedule gaps and venues support later travel/rest features. Actual attendance, observed game weather and postgame reports cannot masquerade as pregame forecasts. No stadium-guest identities or unsupported personal information are fabricated.

Keep source research and transformation choices independent of the 2025-onward outcome results. Evaluate new features using chronological splits wholly inside the development period through 2024 before running a predeclared holdout evaluation.
