# Tennis conditions source research, 2026-09-25

No datasets were fetched and no collector was added for this research.

The original [serve-and-volley publisher](https://github.com/serve-and-volley/atp-world-tour-tennis-data) labels its work CC BY 4.0. Its [producer code](https://github.com/serve-and-volley/atp-world-tour-tennis-data/blob/master/python/tournaments.py) extracts tournament start dates, nominal indoor/outdoor conditions, and surface from ATP results archives. Repository metadata at commit `a92c6192c5e775df9d6be81d3ef978052b012cd9` lists three tournament CSVs for 2000–2009, 2010–2019 and 2020–2022 totaling 604,235 bytes. Their actual rows were not inspected. No 2023–2024 tournament files were listed. These are tournament designations, not measured court pace or match-level roof operation. Underlying ATP content rights remain unresolved: the [ATP terms](https://www.atptour.com/en/terms-and-conditions-app) restrict systematic retrieval. The repository's license alone does not resolve this conflict, so this is not an approved ingestion source.

[USGS 3DEP](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services) offers public-domain terrain elevation. The [official point-query service documentation](https://epqs.nationalmap.gov/v1/docs), linked by the [USGS download guide](https://www.usgs.gov/the-national-map-data-delivery/gis-data-download), makes a small US-venue elevation pilot feasible without full raster downloads. It would need reviewed venue coordinates and explicit source/resolution/datum metadata. Current terrain elevation is not surveyed court height or evidence of what was available before a historical match; pre-2025 acquisition/publication would require separate verification. No point queries were made.

[ITF surface classification](https://www.itftennis.com/en/about-us/tennis-tech/classified-surfaces/) concerns surface products, rather than actual pace at a particular match. No explicit reusable historical table of tournament ball models, match roof operations, sessions or umpire assignments was verified. Current product lists and generic news reports should not be substituted for dated observations. The previously excluded tennis-data.co.uk, Open-Meteo and Overpass sources were not retried.
